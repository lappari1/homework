# h4 Feed me

## x) Why would you want to use a feed reader
- A feed reader makes following multiple websites and blogs easier because all updates are collected in one place. Instead of manually visiting each website to check for new posts, the reader automatically retrieves new content from subscribed RSS or Atom feeds.

## y) Supreme court of Finland, case Osuuspankkikeskus port scan KKO:2003:36. Have a brief look, summarize the key thing in a couple of bullets or sentences
- The case involved a person who used a port scanning tool to scan Osuuspankkikeskus network and search for open proxy servers and potential vulnerabilities
- The port scan did not bypass the firewall, so access to the system was never achieved
- The Supreme Court considered the action an attempted computer intrusion (attempted hacking) because the scanning was intended to find weaknesses that could enable unauthorized access
- The case demonstrates that even attempting to identify security weaknesses in someone else's network can be a criminal offence if the intention is to gain unauthorized access
Source: https://www.finlex.fi/fi/oikeuskaytanto/korkein-oikeus/ennakkopaatokset/2003/36
