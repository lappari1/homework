# a) Listen to podcast and summarize it
Darknet Diaries: ZeuS
    - ZeuS is banking trojan designed to steal money from online bank user's accounts
    - Became so big that it resulted in one of the biggest FBI operations ever
    - Coded by young 22 yo Russian guy, used multiple names but in the end settled into one name, Slavic
    - Started under name WSN Poem in 2006 and by December 2007, hackers had stoen over 200k $ from commercial bank accounts across, UK, USA, Italy and Spain
    - It worked this way: Hackers were sending malware through spam emails and drive by downloads getting it to many machines as possible. Once installed it seeked out all the credentials stored on their computer. After that it would wait for users to log in to their online bank account and as soon as it did, the malware alerted the hackers who would then jump into the session, get on that users computer and transfer money from the user's account to their accounts.
    - Early success proved to the hackers that they were on to something big and if they could improve the malware a little bit and scale it up, they could steal a lot of money this way.
    - They continued to develop the malware and their skills and after six months they rolled out new malware called Z Bot, short for Zeus Bot. 
    - Zeus would not only steal bank credentials and money, but it also infected the computer and made it join a botnet, a giant network of infected machines. 
    - Slavic was very passionate about Zeus, he updated it and added new features regularly.
    - He built it like DYI hackers tool kit that could be sold on dark web to the people who wanted to built their own botnets
    - Many other hackers and hacker groups bought Zeus and used it on their own purposes, but mainly for stealing money from the online bank user's accounts
    - By May 2009 FBI was starting to receive reports of large scale bank transfers that were fraudulently sent but had no evidence of a security breach
    - It was very strange to them that there was no traces of suspicious of IP's or anything, but the stolen money was just transferred by the customer from the customers IP at the home at the customers very browser
    - That made FBI wonder if the customers were just trying to commit a fraud and lying that the money was stolen
    - In 2009 Slavic decided to team up with hacker group called Avalanche and they launched even more powerful version of Zeus called Jabber Zeus
    - Jabber Zeus came with more advanced add on and modules, which they sold with different prices, Jabber Zeus could be used to even get two factor authentication code
    - For example an add on that only notified hackers if bank account had x amount of money so they could only target big fishes
    - Slavic and his friends started to target corporate accounts with hundreds of thousands of dollars in them
    - For this they needed so called money mules, people who are willing to act go betweens, a point between the fraud and the thieves
    - They advertised a job on online job board like Craigslist, obviously they don't advertise money mule for a hire :D
    - They posted a job for a writer or someone to do some clerical work at home
    - Then they ask them to commit a crime, but the person doesn't realize it's a crime
    - They would say something like "we need to pay one of our suppliers, but our bank is having problems. Is it possible for us to send you the money and then you write a check to them? And by the way you can keep 5 % of the money as a bonus for helping us"
    - Then the "mule" get the stolen money added to their account, they write a check for a little less than the full amount to go to the thieves account or another money mules account. And the reason why this is illegal is because the money mule is laundering money by taking stolen funds and passing it along.
    - Lots of people were tricked into money mules
