## a) Process to find and choose article

I used following search term on Google Scholar: intitle:"review" and "social engineering"

It caught my interest because it was published this year in the Journal of Cybersecurity, which has a JUFO rating of 1.

I also find social engineering an interesting topic because it exploits human psychology rather than just technical vulnerabilities.

## b) Review of the review

- What are the key areas being researched?
    - Six categories of social engineering countermeasures, including employee training, technical controls and security policies
    - Countermeasures across three phases: before attack, during attack and after attack
    - Combining different measures into a coordinated security strategy
- What's widely agreed upon?
    - Not a single countermeasure is sufficient alone, multiple measures are needed
    - Employee training and technical controls are both important
    - Organizations should check if their security measures work and learn from past attack
- What are the open questions?
    - How can organizations learn better from incidents?
    - How effective are AI-based detection methods?
    - How can different security measures work better together?
